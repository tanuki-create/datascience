# Web Security Glossary Video Series Design

## Style Prompt

Vertical Japanese micro-lessons with a calm security-lab feel: crisp white canvas, terminal-like code details, bright risk markers, and diagram-first explanations. The viewer should understand the term from abbreviation, origin, and the lower technical layer without needing audio.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#F7F9F6`
- Ink: `#17211A`
- Muted text: `#414A42`
- Risk red: `#E6462E`
- Trust green: `#1E9F70`
- Packet cyan: `#0B6F78`
- Warning amber: `#F2B84B`

## Typography

- Primary: generic `sans-serif` so HyperFrames can render consistently across environments
- Code: generic `monospace`

## Motion

- Use quick, readable scene changes, not decorative motion.
- Diagrams enter after the spoken/text idea is introduced.
- Captions stay in the lower safe zone but never touch the bottom UI area.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use a dark-blue cyber theme.
- Do not rely on dense paragraphs inside the frame.
- Do not make the lesson depend on audio only.
- Do not show generic stock-security imagery.
- Do not place critical text in the bottom 260px social UI zone.
