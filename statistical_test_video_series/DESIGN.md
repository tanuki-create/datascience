# Statistical Test Video Series Design

## Style Prompt

Vertical Japanese micro-lessons with a clean statistical notebook feel: white lab canvas, measured grid, compact formulas, clear group comparisons, and animated dots/distribution curves. The viewer should understand which data shape maps to which test statistic without audio.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#F8FAF7`
- Ink: `#18201A`
- Muted text: `#435047`
- Sample blue: `#2166AC`
- Difference magenta: `#B7357A`
- Rank amber: `#C78318`
- Decision green: `#168A5A`
- Warning red: `#B83A2D`

## Typography

- Primary: generic `sans-serif` for stable HyperFrames rendering
- Formula: generic `monospace`

## Motion

- Dots move only to clarify grouping, pairing, ranking, or cumulative comparison.
- Formula cards should enter after the data shape is visible.
- Captions remain readable and never compete with formulas.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not make a dark cyber or finance-dashboard theme.
- Do not show dense textbook paragraphs.
- Do not imply p値 alone proves the effect size.
- Do not collapse Mann-Whitney into a simplistic “median test” explanation.
- Do not make ANOVA look like it identifies which pair differs by itself.
